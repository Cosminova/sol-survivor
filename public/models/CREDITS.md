# 3D model credits

- `nasa-astronaut.glb`: "Astronaut" (Extravehicular Mobility Unit) from NASA 3D Resources,
  https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Models/Astronaut. NASA media is
  generally not copyrighted (see https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Unmodified file;
  split into limb segments at runtime by `src/world3d/suitModel.js`.
- `draco/`: Draco glTF decoder from three.js r185 (MIT), bundled for offline loading.
